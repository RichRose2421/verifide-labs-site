Why you can trust these numbers

One book, one venue, never blended. Every close posts publicly as it happens — losers included. Trades close one of three ways: a protective stop, a profit target, or a time stop (a maximum holding window, disclosed openly).

Behind the book is a research process built to kill its own ideas: every hypothesis is pre-registered and cryptographically hashed before it touches data, tested against held-out data it never saw, and corrected for every candidate we ever tried. Most ideas die. We publish the graveyard.
